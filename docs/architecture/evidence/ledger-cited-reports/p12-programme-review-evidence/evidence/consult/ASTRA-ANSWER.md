What is missing is a consistently owned and carried destination requirement across the composed routes. The design already names target declaration selection as the owner of a stable declaration requirement. [Authoritative records](/home/losses/Development/tq-workspace/boring-wt-architecture/docs/compiler-policy-interfaces.md:17).
